Night Vault — Privacy Policy

Developer: ShivamAppStudio  ·  Package: com.shivamappstudio.nightvault

Last updated: September 13, 2026

The short version: Night Vault stores everything locally on your device. We collect no personal data, operate no servers, and have no cloud backend. Nothing leaves your phone. Ever.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Night Vault Android application ("App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use it. Night Vault is developed and published by mShivam | App Studio.

By installing or using Night Vault, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the app.

2. Information We Collect

Night Vault does not collect, store on any server, transmit, or sell any personal information.

The app operates entirely offline. Specifically:

  • No user account is created or required.
  • No email address, phone number, or identity is collected.
  • No analytics, crash reporting SDKs, or advertising SDKs are included.
  • No usage statistics are sent to any server.
  • No location data is collected.

All data you store in the vault — photos, videos, audio, passwords, and contacts — resides exclusively in your device's private app directory, encrypted at rest.

4. How Your Data Is Protected

Night Vault uses industry-standard encryption to protect all content stored in the vault:

  • AES-256-GCM chunk encryption is applied to every file stored in the vault.
  • The vault database is protected using SQLCipher, which encrypts all database records at rest.
  • Authentication is enforced via a user-defined PIN or device biometrics before any vault content can be accessed.
  • The FLAG_SECURE window flag is applied throughout the app, preventing the Android system and third-party apps from capturing screenshots of vault content.
  • All vault files are stored in the app's private internal directory and are not visible to file managers, the system gallery, or other apps.

We do not hold or have access to your encryption keys. Your PIN and biometric credentials are never transmitted anywhere.

5. Third-Party Services

Night Vault does not integrate any of the following:

  • Analytics platforms (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude)
  • Crash reporting tools (e.g., Crashlytics, Sentry, Bugsnag)
  • Advertising networks (e.g., AdMob, Meta Audience Network)
  • Cloud storage services (e.g., Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud)
  • Social login SDKs

There are no third-party SDKs in Night Vault that collect or transmit user data.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

All data stored by Night Vault lives on your device only.

  • To delete all vault data: Go to Android Settings → Apps → Night Vault → Storage → Clear Data. This permanently deletes all encrypted files, passwords, and contacts stored in the vault.
  • Uninstalling the app also removes all associated vault data from your device.

Because we hold no data on any server, we have nothing to delete on our end. There is no "account deletion" process because there is no account.

7. Children's Privacy

Night Vault is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Because the app collects no personal data from any user, it poses no particular risk to children's privacy. However, we recommend parental guidance for young users.
